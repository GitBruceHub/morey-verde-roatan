Points of Interest Accessible Within Minutes of Your Door

Living at Morey Verde | Vista Azul places you in the center of everything that makes Roatán one of the Caribbean's most sought-after destinations. Within minutes, you can be relaxing on the white sands of West Bay Beach, exploring the Mesoamerican Barrier Reef, or diving world-famous sites like the Blue Channel, Mary's Place, Texas & Pablos, West End Wall, and the island's 150+ marked dive locations. Adventure seekers will appreciate proximity to South Shore Zipline, where canopy rides offer panoramic jungle and ocean views, as well as the popular eco-experiences at Gumbalimba Park and Daniel Johnson's Sloth Hangout.

For those who enjoy exploring beyond the beach, scenic drives lead to Camp Bay Beach on the island's east end, while cultural excursions through the Oakridge Mangrove Tunnels provide a glimpse into Roatán's historic fishing communities. Evening outings are equally convenient, whether enjoying sunset cocktails at the Roatán Island Brewery or waterfront dining throughout West End village. The surrounding area blends marine biodiversity, adventure tourism, and relaxed island living into one cohesive lifestyle.
